# Techspirex growth motion

## The commercial bet

Techspirex sells considered, high-trust work. Buyers will inspect the team, the proof, and the handoff before they book a call. The strongest motion pairs useful public evidence with selective direct outreach.

The site should earn attention by helping a buyer make a real decision. Sales can then send the same evidence into live opportunities. One asset does both jobs.

## GTM motion scores

| Motion | Fit | Role now |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Inbound | 9/10 | Primary. Publish narrow decision guides, technical investigations, and inspectable work. |
| Outbound | 8/10 | Primary. Send one relevant proof asset to a small, researched account list. |
| ABM | 7/10 | Secondary. Build account-specific proof paths once the outbound message converts. |
| Partners | 6/10 | Secondary. Develop relationships with design studios, fractional product leaders, and cloud specialists. |
| Community | 5/10 | Supporting. Contribute useful technical judgement where founders and product leaders already gather. |
| Paid digital | 4/10 | Later. Retarget proven visitors and defend high-intent branded search. Do not pay to discover the message. |
| PLG | 3/10 | Weak fit for a service business. Product labs can prove craft, but they do not replace a sales conversation. |

Recommended stack: inbound plus selective outbound. Add ABM after the first message and asset pair produces qualified conversations. Add partners after the delivery process has enough disclosed proof for a third party to recommend with confidence.

## Primary loop

The first loop is a practical-value content loop:

1. A founder faces a recurring delivery decision.
2. Search, outreach, or a peer surfaces a focused Techspirex field guide.
3. The guide resolves part of the decision without demanding an email address.
4. The reader downloads or forwards the branded card.
5. A peer follows the Techspirex trail to the full guide and shipped work.
6. A qualified visitor sends a brief or books a call.
7. New sales questions become the next field guide.

The team-shape guide is the first cycle. It connects a common buying question to Techspirex's commercial model: specialist, pod, complete team, or intervention.

No loop coefficient should be projected before the first thirty days of observed traffic. Track it with:

`field-guide viewers x download or share rate x referred-visitor activation rate`

## Ninety-day execution

### Days 1-30

- Launch the team-shape guide on the homepage and as a downloadable share card.
- Add GA4 events for guide views, downloads, insight clicks, brief starts, and booked-call clicks.
- Send the guide to a small set of founders or product leaders currently deciding between hiring and outsourcing.
- Record the questions and objections that follow.

Success signal: qualified readers reach shipped work or begin a contact path. Raw traffic alone does not count.

### Days 31-60

- Publish two more field guides from live sales questions: scope before build, and human checkpoints in AI automation.
- Give each guide one branded share card and one direct route to relevant work.
- Build three outbound sequences, each anchored to one buyer problem and one guide.
- Test partner distribution with five fractional product leaders or specialist studios.

Success signal: one guide reliably opens a conversation with its intended buyer.

### Days 61-90

- Compare guide-assisted leads by quality, not volume.
- Turn the strongest guide into a deeper working session or live teardown.
- Build account-specific proof paths for the best-performing segment.
- Retarget only visitors who reached a guide and a work page. Keep paid spend small until the contact rate holds.

Success signal: the same narrow message works in inbound discovery, direct outreach, and partner referral.

## Measurement

Track weekly:

- Guide landing-page views by source
- Share-card download rate
- Insight-to-work click rate
- Work-to-contact-path rate
- Brief starts and call clicks assisted by a guide
- Qualified conversations per 100 target accounts
- Opportunities influenced by partners
- Time from first guide visit to contact

The loop earns more investment when it produces better conversations. Loud analytics cannot carry the case.

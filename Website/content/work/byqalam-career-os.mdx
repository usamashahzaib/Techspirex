Techspirex designed and engineered [byQalam](https://www.byqalam.com), a live
career technology product. The work covered the product interface, application

[Open the live product](https://www.byqalam.com)

## What it is

byQalam is a career-visibility platform - a workspace that aligns a
professional's LinkedIn profile, published content, and ATS resume around a
single, credible story, explicitly **without fabricating accomplishments**. It is
built for professionals, founders, and teams who want to be found, trusted, and
shortlisted on the strength of what they have actually done.

## What we built

- **LinkedIn profile audit and positioning** - structured analysis that turns a
  profile into a coherent professional narrative.
- **AI post generation with voice training** - a drafting system that learns a
  user's writing patterns from their edits and approved drafts over time, so the
  output reads like them rather than like generic AI.
- **ATS resume review and targeted resumes** - resume tooling that checks against
  applicant-tracking realities and tailors output to a specific role.
- **A Career Vault** - a store of verified experiences and skills that every other
  surface (posts, resumes, cover letters) draws from.
- **Content planning, scheduling, and analytics** - the operating loop for
  publishing consistently, not just once.

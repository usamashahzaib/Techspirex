Techspirex designed and engineered [byQalam](https://www.byqalam.com), a career
technology product that is live in production. The work covered the product
interface, application logic, AI-assisted workflows, user data, authentication,
and billing.

[**Open byQalam (byqalam.com)**](https://www.byqalam.com)

## What it is

byQalam is a career-visibility platform: a workspace that aligns a professional's
LinkedIn profile, published content, and ATS resume around one consistent story.

## What we built

- **LinkedIn profile audit and positioning** - structured analysis that turns a
  profile into a coherent professional narrative.
- **AI post generation with voice training** - a drafting system that learns a
  user's writing patterns from their edits and approved drafts over time.
- **ATS resume review and targeted resumes** - resume tooling that checks against
  applicant-tracking requirements and tailors output to a specific role.
- **A Career Vault** - a store of experiences and skills that posts, resumes, and
  cover letters draw from.
- **Content planning, scheduling, and analytics** - the operating loop for
  publishing consistently.

## What this project shows

Authentication, billing, AI workflows that improve with use, and a persistent data
model behind every feature, delivered as one product and running in production.

Performance metrics such as signups, revenue, and retention are not published here.
